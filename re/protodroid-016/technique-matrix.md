# Technique matrix

No technique is ranked or adopted before the Phase 2 source audit and Phase 3 ProtoDroid profiling. The eventual matrix uses these columns:

| Technique/source | Concrete implementation | Expected benefit | Evidence confidence | Correctness/device risk | Cost | Validation | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Decisions are `best candidate`, `uncertain`, `rejected`, `implemented pending device`, or `adopted with controlled evidence`. A technique is not “best” merely because another project uses it. Frame generation, upscaling, hidden quality reductions, and feature removal are not eligible as real engine-FPS optimizations.
